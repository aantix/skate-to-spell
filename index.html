The Ice Rule ❄️
When c or g comes right before e, i, or y, it gets soft:
c says /s/ → face, cell, center, space
g says /j/ → germ, page, digit
Everywhere else they're hard: carpet, gray.
Tricky word: are (you just have to know it!)
